# Exercise 14.2: Revenue by Invoice Date and by Ship Date

{{ header() }}

## Purpose and learning outcomes

Chapter 12's auditor found invoices posted in the year after the goods were delivered. The controller now wants each year's revenue by invoice date beside its revenue by ship date, and the lines that cross a year listed. The exercise is the inactive relationship at work: one Date table, two roles, and a measure that chooses. It also practices flattening, because ShippedLines carries its header's and its shipment's dates. The analytics type is descriptive, with a first step toward diagnosis.

Students can:

- build a fact table from three merges and keep a left join where one side may be missing (Requirements 1 and 2);
- relate a second date inactively and say why it cannot be active (Requirement 3);
- switch the relationship on in a measure and explain the (Blank) row (Requirement 4);
- flag and sort the lines that cross a year and tie them to Chapter 12 (Requirement 5);
- recommend a revenue date and say what to disclose (Requirement 6).

**Competencies practiced.** CPA Evolution BAR M10 (advanced data analytics: data models and joins) and Core S1 M1 to M3 (financial statements, accounts and transactions); IMA Reporting and Control (financial recordkeeping, financial statement preparation) and Data Analytics and Insights (data tools and techniques); IFAC IES 2 (a) (financial accounting and reporting) and IES 2 (i) (using information and communications technologies to analyze data); AACSB A5 part 5.1.

## Before you assign

- **Files.** The copy after Tutorial 14.3, with SalesInvoice kept as a query that does not load. The model answer is page Ex 14.2, the three queries and the folder Ex 14.2 of Key Measures; the Checks tab holds the values.
- **Data conditions.** SalesInvoiceLine.ShipmentLineID is empty on the {{ g.n_services|count }} Services lines, because design services do not ship; every other line finds its ShipmentLine and Shipment. Every shipment is delivered in the month it ships. No line is dated outside the Date table.
- **Verify first.** In **Model view**, the copy has SalesInvoiceLine related to Date on InvoiceDate. ShippedLines is a second fact table, so the two share Date and nothing else.
- **A harmless oddity.** The first Changed Type step of ShippedLines types Discount as a whole number, Chapter 13's trap. The next step drops the column, so nothing is wrong.

### Requirement (6): The recommendation

**Step 6. Outline the memo.** Use the ship date for goods revenue. Chapter 12 assumes that control passes on delivery, and every shipment is delivered in the month it ships, so the ship date dates revenue as delivery would; invoice-date revenue misstates the years by the late invoices. Services have no ship date, so the report needs a rule, such as the invoice date as a documented proxy for when the work was done, and it should disclose the invoice-date figure beside it. Accept any recommendation that names its date, gives the Services rule and discloses the other.

## What a strong answer says

- **(3)** The pair of tables has an active path already, so the second date is inactive; a measure switches it on.
- **(4)** The tie of Shipped Revenue to the Validation page, and the (Blank) row as the Services lines that do not ship.
- **(5)** The two kinds of crossing, each with its cause, and the comparison with Chapter 12: a different date basis, and one invoice more.
- **(6)** A date, the Services rule, the disclosure, and the assumption that control passes on delivery.

A strong answer ties each figure to a table and says which date it uses. A weak one chooses a date by preference.

## Common mistakes and how to respond

- **An inner join.** *Symptom:* Shipped Revenue short of Revenue by the Services amount each year. *Response:* ask which lines have no shipment, and which join keeps them.
- **No USERELATIONSHIP.** *Symptom:* the two columns agree. *Response:* ask which relationship the measure uses.
- **A guardless flag.** *Symptom:* {{ g.n_noguard|count }} lines in the table. *Response:* ask what the Services lines' ship date is.
- **The (Blank) row treated as an error.** *Symptom:* a plan to delete it. *Response:* ask what a design service shipping date would be.
- **A difference from Chapter 12.** *Symptom:* "the model disagrees with the audit". *Response:* ask which date each one uses.
- **A recommendation by preference.** *Symptom:* "the ship date is more accurate". *Response:* ask what assumption makes it so.
