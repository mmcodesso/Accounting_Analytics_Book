# Exercise 13.3: Promotions in the Report

{{ header() }}

## Purpose and learning outcomes

The marketing manager wants the promotions by name, with what each gave away and when, before the next calendar is discussed. The exercise brings a fifth table into the model and then reads it carefully: a row labeled (Blank) is a meaningful blank and not a broken key, invoices dated after a promotion's end date are not violations, and one end date is simply wrong. It ends where the report stops: the cost of a promotion is on the page and its payoff is not. The analytics type is descriptive, with the first step toward diagnosis (where the discounts fell).

Students can:

- import and relate a lookup table whose key was typed with care (Requirement 1);
- build a matrix with two values and read a (Blank) row correctly (Requirement 2);
- compare invoice dates with a promotion's dates without mistaking billing for pricing (Requirement 3);
- spot a master-data error in a date (Requirement 4);
- state what the report shows and what it cannot (Requirement 5).

**Competencies practiced.** CPA Evolution Core S1 M8 (visualization and communicating results; data mining and analysis); IMA Data Analytics and Insights (data visualization) and Strategy, Planning and Performance (performance management); IFAC IES 2 (b) (management accounting) and IES 3 (b) (communication); AACSB A5 part 5.1.

## What a strong answer says

- **(2)** The (Blank) row is the lines sold without a promotion, with revenue and no discount: a meaningful blank, not a defect.
- **(3)** The months after each end date, and why they are not errors: pricing happens at the order. Chapter 5's finding about the orders is the evidence.
- **(4)** The promotion by name, its dates, and why its lines still carry it: their orders were priced with the promotion. A strong answer adds the likely season from the order months and names the approved promotion as the record that would settle the dates.
- **(5)** Dollars, months and groups from the matrices, with the limit stated: cost without lift or margin.

## Common mistakes and how to respond

- **No year.** *Symptom:* {{ g.promos_all }} promotions and a (Blank) row of {{ g.blank_all|count }} lines. *Response:* ask which year the title promises.
- **A key of the wrong type.** *Symptom:* no relationship, or every line under (Blank). *Response:* ask what type the icon on PromotionID shows in each table.
- **(Blank) as a defect.** *Symptom:* a request to "fix" the row. *Response:* ask what a line sold without a promotion should show.
- **Late lines as violations.** *Symptom:* a memo that reports discounts outside the window. *Response:* ask when the order was priced.
- **Cost reported as payoff.** *Symptom:* "the Furniture promotion worked". *Response:* ask what volume the report shows.
