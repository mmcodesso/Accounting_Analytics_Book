## Purpose and learning outcomes

Chapter 12 found sales invoices for December deliveries recorded in January, and December shipments never invoiced. The exercise adds both tests to a copy of the register, as a monitor would, and then asks a question that decides what a test can see: which date counts as the invoice's year. The tests compare the year of delivery with the year of the invoice, so they test the cutoff at a year-end, not between months. The analytics type is diagnostic.

Students can:

- build a test from several tables by merging them (Requirements 1 and 2);
- turn a test into the register's five columns and append it to a copy of the register (Requirements 3 and 4);
- show a new test by month and relate it to its own table of tests (Requirement 4);
- compare two definitions of an invoice's year and explain the invoice they split (Requirement 5).

**Competencies practiced.** CPA Evolution Core S2 M9 (audit procedures and audit data analytics); IMA Reporting and Control (internal control and financial recordkeeping); IFAC IES 2 (a) (financial accounting and reporting) and (f) (audit); AACSB A5 part 5.1.

## Step-by-step solution

### Requirement (1): Shipment lines with their delivery date

**Step 1. Import and merge.**

a. Choose **Home > Get data > Excel workbook**, select T14_Shipment and T15_ShipmentLine, and choose **Transform Data**.
b. Rename them `CutoffShipments` and `CutoffShipmentLines`, keep the columns the exercise names, and set DeliveryDate to **Date**.
c. In CutoffShipmentLines, choose **Home > Merge Queries** with CutoffShipments on ShipmentID and expand DeliveryDate without the prefix.

```
    #"Merged Queries" = Table.NestedJoin(#"Removed Other Columns", {"ShipmentID"}, CutoffShipments, {"ShipmentID"}, "CutoffShipments", JoinKind.LeftOuter),
    #"Expanded CutoffShipments" = Table.ExpandTableColumn(#"Merged Queries", "CutoffShipments", {"DeliveryDate"}, {"DeliveryDate"})
```

**Check:** every shipment line carries a delivery date. The two source queries do not load (**Enable load** cleared).

### Requirement (2): Invoice lines with the invoice and the delivery

**Step 2. Merge the invoice and the delivery onto the invoice lines.**

a. Import T17_SalesInvoiceLine as `CutoffInvoiceLines` (ShipmentLineID and SalesInvoiceID) and T16_SalesInvoice as `CutoffInvoices` (SalesInvoiceID, InvoiceNumber, InvoiceDate, SubTotal).
b. Merge CutoffInvoices onto the lines on SalesInvoiceID and expand InvoiceNumber, InvoiceDate and SubTotal.
c. Merge CutoffShipmentLines on ShipmentLineID and expand DeliveryDate.

```
    #"Merged Queries" = Table.NestedJoin(#"Removed Other Columns", {"SalesInvoiceID"}, CutoffInvoices, {"SalesInvoiceID"}, "CutoffInvoices", JoinKind.LeftOuter),
    #"Expanded CutoffInvoices" = Table.ExpandTableColumn(#"Merged Queries", "CutoffInvoices", {"InvoiceNumber", "InvoiceDate", "SubTotal"}, {"InvoiceNumber", "InvoiceDate", "SubTotal"}),
    #"Merged Queries1" = Table.NestedJoin(#"Expanded CutoffInvoices", {"ShipmentLineID"}, CutoffShipmentLines, {"ShipmentLineID"}, "CutoffShipmentLines", JoinKind.LeftOuter),
    #"Expanded CutoffShipmentLines" = Table.ExpandTableColumn(#"Merged Queries1", "CutoffShipmentLines", {"DeliveryDate"}, {"DeliveryDate"})
```

**Check:** one row per invoice line, with the invoice's number, date and subtotal and the line's delivery date. **Watch for:** the design-service lines have no delivery date: that is correct, not a failed merge.

### Requirement (3): The two exception queries

**Step 3. Build SI DeliveredPriorYear.**

a. Reference CutoffInvoiceLines as `SI DeliveredPriorYear`, and choose **Transform > Group By**, select **Advanced**, group by InvoiceNumber, InvoiceDate and SubTotal, and name the latest DeliveryDate `LastDelivery`.
b. Keep the rows whose LastDelivery year is before the InvoiceDate year.
c. Add `TestID` and an empty `EmployeeID`, rename the columns to the register's, and keep the five.

```
    #"Grouped Rows" = Table.Group(Source, {"InvoiceNumber", "InvoiceDate", "SubTotal"}, {{"LastDelivery", each List.Max([DeliveryDate]), type nullable date}}),
    #"Filtered Rows" = Table.SelectRows(#"Grouped Rows", each [LastDelivery] <> null and Date.Year([LastDelivery]) < Date.Year([InvoiceDate])),
```

**Step 4. Build SL NotInvoiced.**

a. Reference CutoffShipmentLines as `SL NotInvoiced` and merge it with CutoffInvoiceLines on ShipmentLineID, choosing the join kind that keeps only the rows with no match (left anti).
b. Remove the merged column, add `TestID`, a `DocumentNumber` built from the line's key and an empty `EmployeeID`, rename DeliveryDate and ExtendedStandardCost to `EventDate` and `Amount`, and keep the five columns.

```
    #"Merged Queries" = Table.NestedJoin(Source, {"ShipmentLineID"}, CutoffInvoiceLines, {"ShipmentLineID"}, "CutoffInvoiceLines", JoinKind.LeftAnti),
```

**Check:** SI DeliveredPriorYear returns 22 invoices, 15 dated in January 2025 and 7 dated in January 2026, subtotal $106,446.61; SL NotInvoiced returns 9 shipment lines, $22,895.78 at standard cost, all December 2026 deliveries. **Watch for:** filtering the lines before grouping. The test then returns 23 rows for 22 invoices and counts $127,490.59. Testing the earliest line in place of the latest gives the same 22 here, because no invoice has lines delivered in two years, so that error survives until a roll.

### Requirement (4): The register copy and the page

**Step 5. Append, describe and relate.**

a. Choose **Home > Append Queries as New**, select **Three or more tables**, add Exceptions, SI DeliveredPriorYear and SL NotInvoiced, and name the result `Exceptions 16.2`. Set EventDate to **Date**.
b. Choose **Home > Enter data**, create `Tests 16.2` with the eleven rows of Tests and two more, one for each new test (process: revenue cutoff), and choose **Load**.

d. Add the measure below to the display folder Ex 16.2, and build a matrix of Date YearMonth on **Rows**, TestID of Tests 16.2 on **Columns** and the measure as **Values**, filtered to the two new tests, titled "The two cutoff tests by month". Add a table titled "The documents".

```
    Source = Table.Combine({Exceptions, #"SI DeliveredPriorYear", #"SL NotInvoiced"}),
```

```

```

**Check:** Exceptions 16.2 holds 182 rows and Tests 16.2 13. The matrix shows the invoices in January of 2025 and 2026 and the shipment lines in December 2026. **Watch for:** three failures. Relating to the original Tests leaves 31 exceptions with no test. The tutorial's Exceptions measure counts the old table, so every row reads 151. A rename left out leaves EventDate empty, and the same 31 rows sit under no month.

### Requirement (5): The extra invoice

**Step 6. Find it in the ledger.**

a. In ARLedger, filter VoucherNumber to the invoice that Chapter 12 counted in January 2026, or read the instructor check below.
b. Compare its posting date with its invoice, shipment and delivery dates.

**Check:** SI-2026-013742 is dated in December 2025, shipped on 2025-12-06, delivered on 2025-12-10 and posted on 2026-01-01. By posting date it joins January 2026, so Chapter 12 counts 15 and 8, and the test counts 15 and 7. By its own date it falls in the year of its delivery, so this test cannot see it. It is Chapter 6's revenue cutoff error: revenue recorded in the year after the goods shipped. Five other invoices are dated before their delivery (SI-2025-006733 to 006735, SI-2026-013741, SI-2026-013743), the opposite direction, not asked here.

## What a strong answer says

- **(3)** Both tests with their counts, and the reason they compare years: a cutoff error shows only across a year-end.
- **(5)** The extra invoice, its four dates, and the rule that a test depends on the date it uses: by invoice date the invoice is in its delivery year, by posting date it is not. A strong answer adds that a monitor should state which date it tests, and may run both.

A strong answer names the invoice and the dates. A weak one says "one more invoice".

## Common mistakes and how to respond

- **Lines, not invoices.** *Symptom:* 23 rows. *Response:* ask what one row should stand for.
- **The wrong Tests table.** *Symptom:* 31 exceptions with no test. *Response:* ask which table the new TestID values live in.
- **The old measure.** *Symptom:* 151 on every row. *Response:* ask which table the measure counts.
- **Open invoices as the source.** *Symptom:* fewer than 22 invoices, because the paid ones are gone. *Response:* ask what ARInvoices keeps.
- **A posting-date count in the test.** *Symptom:* 23 invoices. *Response:* ask which date the exercise says to use.
- **A missing rename.** *Symptom:* dates under no month. *Response:* ask what Append matches columns by.
