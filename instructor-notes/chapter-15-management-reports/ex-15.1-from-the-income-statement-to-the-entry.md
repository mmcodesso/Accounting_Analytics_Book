## Purpose and learning outcomes

An income statement answers how much. A reader who sees a line that appears in two years and no others asks what it is, and the report should answer in two clicks. The exercise builds that path: a drill-through page keyed on the account subtype, a read of the entry behind the number, and a query that lists every posting of the entry. The line is 7020 Gain or Loss on Asset Disposal, one disposal in each of 2025 and 2026. The analytics type is descriptive, with a diagnostic habit: do not accept a total until you have seen its entries.

Students can:

- build a drill-through page on a field of the visual they will right-click (Requirement 1);
- drill through from a matrix cell and read the entry (Requirement 2);
- list every posting of one entry in DAX query view, with RELATED (Requirement 3);
- explain a loss on disposal from the accounts of its entry (Requirement 4);
- say which filters a drill-through passes and what a missing one costs (Requirement 5).

**Competencies practiced.** CPA Evolution Core S1 M1 to M3 (financial statements, accounts and transactions) and M8 (visualization and communicating results); IMA Reporting and Control (financial recordkeeping) and Data Analytics and Insights (data visualization); IFAC IES 2 (a) (financial accounting and reporting) and IES 2 (i) (using information and communications technologies to analyze data); AACSB A5 part 5.1 (technology integration).

## Step-by-step solution

### Requirement (1): The Ledger Detail page

**Step 1. Add the page and its drill-through field.**

a. Add a page named `Ledger Detail`.
b. With no visual selected, drag AccountSubType from **Account** into the **Drillthrough** section of the **Visualizations** pane, where it says **Add drillthrough fields here**.
c. Add a **Table** visual with PostingDate, VoucherNumber, SourceDocumentType, AccountNumber and AccountName, and add P&L Amount from **Key Measures**.

```dax
P&L Amount = - [GL Amount]
```

**Check:** Power BI adds a back button by itself. The model answer titles the table "Postings to the drilled-through subtype". Opened from its tab with no filter, a page lists every posting, so it is reached by drill-through. The model answer is saved with its drill-through filter left at the subtype, as after a drill-through on the subtype, so it lists one row for each loss entry: only the loss line of an entry sits in the subtype.

**Watch for:** a table built from the Debit and Credit columns, not the measure. It lists the closes too: JE-2025-000287 (2025-12-31), JE-2026-000296 (2026-12-31). Each close reverses its year's loss, so with the two losses the four rows sum to zero, and a drill-through from the 2026 cell shows 2 rows in place of one. P&L Amount shows only the loss entries because GL Amount filters the closes out.

![A mock of the Ledger Detail page as the model answer is saved, with the subtype passed and no year, so it lists both loss entries.](../figures/chapter-15-management-reports/IF-15-1-A.pdf)

### Requirement (2): The two entries

**Step 2. Drill through from the matrix.**

a. On the Income Statement page, right-click the Other Income or Expense cell of fiscal 2026, and choose **Drillthrough > Ledger Detail**.
b. Read the row, then return with the back button, holding Ctrl as Tutorial 15.1 does.
c. Repeat for fiscal 2025.

**Check:** the 2026 page lists JE-2026-000115, dated 2026-06-05, with P&L Amount -$92,500.00; the 2025 page lists JE-2025-000028, dated 2025-02-12, with -$42,000.00. Both are losses, so both are negative on an income statement. The Checks tab proves the entry numbers, dates and amounts; the right-click, the filters passed and the back button are visible only in Desktop.

### Requirement (3): Every posting of the entry

**Step 3. Query the entry.**

a. Open **DAX query view**, and add a query tab named `Ex 15.1`.
b. Type the query below and choose **Run**.

```dax
// Exercise 15.1 (3): every posting of JE-2026-000115, with the account columns brought by RELATED
EVALUATE
SELECTCOLUMNS (
    FILTER ( GLEntry, GLEntry[VoucherNumber] = "JE-2026-000115" ),
    "PostingDate", GLEntry[PostingDate],
    "AccountNumber", RELATED ( Account[AccountNumber] ),
    "AccountName", RELATED ( Account[AccountName] ),
    "Debit", GLEntry[Debit],
    "Credit", GLEntry[Credit]
)
ORDER BY [Debit] DESC, [Credit] DESC
```

**Check:** 3 rows: the debit to 1186 Accumulated Depreciation - Manufacturing Equipment ($117,500.00), the debit to 7020 ($92,500.00) and the credit to 1185 Manufacturing Equipment ($210,000.00). RELATED works because each posting has one account. Change the voucher to JE-2025-000028 and the query returns 4 rows, one of them the cash received.

**Watch for:** a filter on the account, not the voucher. It returns the loss line alone, one row, and the student cannot explain the loss from it.

### Requirement (4): How the amount was calculated

**Step 4. Write the explanation.** The loss is the asset's cost less its accumulated depreciation less the proceeds, and the entry holds each term: JE-2026-000115 credits 1185 with $210,000.00, debits 1186 with $117,500.00, and received nothing, so the loss is $92,500.00. The entry retires asset 4, "Retirement Of Obsolete Packing Line Conveyor". JE-2025-000028 gives $168,000.00 less $98,000.00 less $28,000.00 of cash, a loss of $42,000.00: a different asset, "Disposal Of Replaced Forklift A", and proceeds that cut the loss.

![The two disposal entries side by side, each with the loss worked out from its own lines.](../figures/chapter-15-management-reports/IF-15-1-B.pdf)

### Requirement (5): Why the year matters

**Step 5. Explain what the click passes.** A drill-through passes every filter of the data point: the row's AccountSubType, which the page's well holds, and the column's Year, which travels with the click although the page's well does not hold it. Without the year the subtype alone selects the account, and the account holds both entries, so the page lists both, which is how the model answer is saved (the Checks row "entries listed without a year filter" proves the two vouchers). A student sees the same by right-clicking the row's label, which carries no year. The closes are absent in every case, because P&L Amount leaves them out.

**Instructor check.** In **DAX query view**, run this and compare it with the Expected results.

```dax
EVALUATE ROW ( "Entry on the 2026 page", CALCULATE ( CONCATENATEX ( FILTER ( VALUES ( GLEntry[VoucherNumber] ), NOT ISBLANK ( [P&L Amount] ) ), GLEntry[VoucherNumber], ", ", GLEntry[VoucherNumber], ASC ), Account[AccountSubType] = "Other Income or Expense", 'Date'[Year] = 2026 ), "Entries without a year filter", CALCULATE ( CONCATENATEX ( FILTER ( VALUES ( GLEntry[VoucherNumber] ), NOT ISBLANK ( [P&L Amount] ) ), GLEntry[VoucherNumber], ", ", GLEntry[VoucherNumber], ASC ), Account[AccountSubType] = "Other Income or Expense" ) )
```

## What a strong answer says

- **(2)** Names both entries with their dates, says each is a loss on the disposal of an asset, and gives the sign.
- **(4)** The three terms of the loss, from the entry's own lines, and the proceeds as the reason the two years differ.
- **(5)** The click carries the column's year as well as the row; without it the page lists both entries; the closes never appear because the measure leaves them out.

A strong answer reads the entry, not the total. A weak one repeats the account name.

## Common mistakes and how to respond

- **No Drillthrough option.** *Symptom:* the right-click menu lacks it. *Response:* ask whether AccountSubType is a field of the matrix the student clicked.
- **Closes on the page.** *Symptom:* four rows, or two on the 2026 page. *Response:* ask which column of the table is not the measure.
- **One row from the query.** *Symptom:* only the loss line. *Response:* ask what the other lines of the entry are.
- **A gain read from a negative.** *Symptom:* "income of $92,500.00". *Response:* ask which side of the entry the loss account is on.
- **A smaller asset blamed.** *Symptom:* "the 2025 loss is smaller because the asset cost less". *Response:* ask which line the 2026 entry does not have.
- **The year called a slicer.** *Symptom:* "the matrix is filtered by year". *Response:* ask what the page would show if the click had come from the row's label.
