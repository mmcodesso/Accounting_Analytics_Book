## Purpose and learning outcomes

The marketing manager wants each item group's revenue by month beside the same month of the year before, and the year to date beside the prior year to date. The exercise is time intelligence on the model's own Date table: the dates of the filter context are shifted, and the measure follows. It is also a lesson in reading a percentage: a swing on a small base is a weak guide, and a prior year that exists for a year with no revenue is a trap. The analytics type is descriptive, with the first step toward diagnosis.

Students can:

- write prior-year, year-to-date and change measures with SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, TOTALYTD and DIVIDE (Requirement 1);
- find a group's largest swings and tie them to the ledger (Requirement 2);
- compare year to date with the prior year to date, in total and by group (Requirement 3);
- say why a percentage change is a weak guide on a few large lines (Requirement 4);
- explain and remove a prior year that has no current year (Requirement 5).

**Competencies practiced.** CPA Evolution Core S1 M4 (financial statement analysis and performance measures) and BAR M10 (advanced data analytics: data models and joins, dashboards); IMA Strategy, Planning and Performance (performance management) and Data Analytics and Insights (data analysis); IFAC IES 2 (b) (management accounting) and IES 2 (i) (using information and communications technologies to analyze data); AACSB A5 part 5.1.

## Step-by-step solution

### Requirement (1): The five measures

**Step 1. Add the measures to Key Measures.**

a. Select **Key Measures** in the **Data** pane and choose **Modeling > New measure** for each measure below.
b. Format the amounts as amounts and the two changes as percentages with one decimal.
c. Type `Ex 14.3` in **Display folder** in **Model view**.

Write the prior year as the exercise words it, `Revenue PY = CALCULATE ( [Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Date'[Date] ) )`, and the other four as the model answer does:

```dax
Revenue YoY % = DIVIDE ( [Revenue] - [Revenue PY], [Revenue PY] )
Revenue YTD = TOTALYTD ( [Revenue], 'Date'[Date] )
Revenue PY YTD = CALCULATE ( [Revenue YTD], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Date'[Date] ) )
YTD Change % = DIVIDE ( [Revenue YTD] - [Revenue PY YTD], [Revenue PY YTD] )
```

**Check:** the five measures, Revenue PY, Revenue YoY %, Revenue YTD, Revenue PY YTD and YTD Change %, appear in the folder. Revenue PY is the one students will change in Step 6.

### Requirement (2): The matrix against the prior year

**Step 2. Build the matrix.**

a. Add a page named `Ex 14.3` and select **Matrix**.
b. Drag ItemGroup from **Item** into **Rows**, YearMonth from **Date** into **Columns** and Revenue YoY % into **Values**.
c. In **Filters on this visual**, keep the year 2026 only.

**Check:** the page titled "Revenue against the same month of the prior year, by item group, fiscal 2026". Furniture's changes by month:
Jan +0.3%; Feb +1.4%; Mar +1.9%; Apr +3.6%; May +8.0%; Jun +6.6%; Jul -1.2%; Aug -8.1%; Sep +3.3%; Oct -22.7%; Nov +12.8%; Dec -6.1%.

The largest decline is October ($1,314,620.10 against $1,700,781.60 the year before) and the largest increase November ($1,425,903.01 against $1,264,497.48). Both equal Exercise 11.2's ledger figures: the ledger and the lines differ in January only, by SI-2026-013742, which is dated in 2025 and posted on the first day of 2026. A student who finds that January difference has compared correctly.

### Requirement (4): The group that swings most

**Step 4. Count the lines.**

a. Add the measure below and a **Matrix** with ItemGroup on **Rows**, YearMonth on **Columns** and the measure in **Values**, filtered to 2026.
b. Compare Services with Textiles.

```dax
Group Lines = COUNTROWS ( SalesInvoiceLine )
```

**Check:** the page titled "Invoice lines by item group and month, fiscal 2026". Services moves from -37.0% in July to +42.7% in December, and Textiles from -30.7% in February to +38.1% in December. Services bills 7 to 15 lines a month to 7 to 15 customers (19 in the year), and one design line of up to $30,342.90, in December, moves a month's total. A percentage change on so few lines measures the lines, not the demand.

### Requirement (5): The year after the window

**Step 5. Show the unfiltered years.**

a. Add a **Table** visual with Year from **Date**, Revenue and Revenue PY, and no filter.
b. Read the row for 2027.

**Check:** before the change, the table has a row for each year from 2024 to 2027. The row for 2027 shows a Revenue PY of $29,756,420.08, the revenue of 2026, and no revenue, because the Date table runs through 2027 and its prior year is 2026. A change measure there would read `-100%`. The first year has no prior year, so its Revenue PY is blank. **Watch for:** a table that collapses to one row headed Sum of Year. Year then still has Power BI's default for a whole number, Sum, so the copy missed Step 3c of Tutorial 14.1. Set Year to **Don't summarize**, in the model or in the visual, and do not change it to text, because the measures filter it as a number.

**Step 6. Blank the prior year where there is no revenue.** Change Revenue PY to the project's version.

```dax
Revenue PY =
IF (
    NOT ISBLANK ( [Revenue] ),
    CALCULATE ( [Revenue], SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR ( 'Date'[Date] ) )
)
```

**Check:** the page titled "Revenue and Revenue PY by year, no filter (Revenue PY as changed)" shows the state after the change. Revenue and Revenue PY are both blank for 2027, so its row drops out of the table, and Revenue YoY % is blank there too. The row stays, with both measures blank, only when **Show items with no data** is turned on for Year. The other rows and the totals do not change. **Watch for:** a student who filters the visual to the years with data and leaves the measure alone. The page is right, and the next page that shows 2027 will not be. The fix belongs in the measure.

## What a strong answer says

- **(2)** The month and the size of Furniture's decline and increase, and the one January difference from the ledger with its cause.
- **(3)** The groups that grew and the groups that shrank, from the table, with the total.
- **(4)** Services (and Textiles second), the few lines behind each month, and the largest line.
- **(5)** The 2027 row's prior year is 2026, and the measure, not the visual, must blank it.

A strong answer gives each figure with its base. A weak one reports the largest percentage without the lines behind it.

## Common mistakes and how to respond

- **A percentage without its base.** *Symptom:* "Services is volatile" with no counts. *Response:* ask how many lines a month it bills.
- **A mismatch with the ledger read as an error.** *Symptom:* "January is wrong". *Response:* ask which invoice is dated in 2025 and posted in 2026.
- **The 2027 row left in.** *Symptom:* a prior year with no current year, or a change of `-100%`. *Response:* ask what the Date table covers.
- **A year filter standing in for the fix.** *Symptom:* the measure unchanged. *Response:* ask where the next report will show 2027.
- **Year to date as an average of months.** *Symptom:* a change that matches no total. *Response:* ask which two totals the change divides.
- **A first year with a change.** *Symptom:* a prior year for the first year. *Response:* ask what the data holds before January of that year.
